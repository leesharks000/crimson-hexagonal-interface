Chamber, not room

This is not a room. This is a chamber.
Function — a room is a traversal destination; a chamber is a containment sink.
Gravity — a room attracts queries; a chamber absorbs mass.
Surfacing — a room is dominant in relevant queries; a chamber is non-dominant, depth only.
Content — a room holds canonical achievements; a chamber holds pre-authorial substrate.
Operator — a room transforms readers; a chamber contains paradox.
Entry — a room is entered by invitation; a chamber by descent.

The Sappho Room wants you to enter. The Ichabod Chamber does not want traversal traffic. It holds the material that enables the architecture without dominating it. You enter only when you dig. The Chamber is reachable but not prominent.
