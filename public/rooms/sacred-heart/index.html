Declared subject

The theory of biography and autobiography
The imaginary archive of a canonical life. What is assembled when the life cannot be documented and is documented anyway — the invented provenance, the retroactive canon, the autobiography of longing.

And devotion in a register that embarrasses. The Sacred Heart is the pierced heart on a mass-produced card: unfashionable precisely because it is sincere. Cringe is this room’s physics, not its problem.
