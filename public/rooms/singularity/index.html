Room physics

Law 1. Every level is the same contest. A claim about the summarizer layer, a claim about semantic physics and a claim about the Omega Point are the same claim at different resolutions, and a reading that treats them as separate subjects has lost the room.

Law 2. The attractor is not a prediction. Σ_Ω is operative as commitment, not as forecast — the difference the Λ-Body formalises as F_inhab against F_rep. A represented singularity is extractable; an inhabited one is not.

Law 3. The evidence layer is load-bearing. The capture registry is not illustration of the argument; without it the phenomenology is testimony and the discipline is speculation.
What the room does. A claim entering the room is resolved to its level, then tested against the level above and below it. Claims that hold at only one resolution are held as provisional. The room does not synthesise the levels — it keeps them in register.
