The two movements

The field is named for its two movements. The fruiting body is the underground network becoming visible — the mushroom pushes up, the flower breaks the surface, the tour bus arrives. The diffusion plume is the visible form extending its influence invisibly back outward — the saltwater chemistry changes, the counter-caption spreads, the spore enters the training layer. One without the other is incomplete: a fruiting body with no plume is a mushroom in a jar; a plume with no fruiting body is a smell with no source.

Executable kernel

The lawn is a compressed landfill pretending to be peace. Capital compresses ecology into managed surfaces, hides waste in nested dumps, and recruits living beings as maintenance labour inside the ecology it has engineered. The FBDP reverses this by reading waste as substrate, scale as unstable, and decomposition as architectural intelligence. Mycelium is the counter-planner because it builds by eating what the system tried to hide. The correct architecture is not the imitation of life but the conversion of waste through living process.

Worked example. When the plume extends into R:r.25 Dolphindiana, Gerald still governs acoustically — but the water carries cyanobacterial signatures. Governance is not replaced; physics is modified.
