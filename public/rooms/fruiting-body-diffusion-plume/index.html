The two movements

A self-executable field specification and ecosystem graph.

CONTAINER TYPE: FIELD A field is a new container class in the Crimson Hexagonal Archive. Unlike a room (which contains), a chamber (which holds paradox), a vault (which prevents extraction), or a portal (which connects), a field has no walls. It has a source and a gradient. It does not hold a traverser inside — it changes the traverser's environment as they pass through it. A room can be inside a field. The field does not replace the room; it modifies the room's physics. The field is defined not by its boundaries but by its radius of influence and the rate at which that influence attenuates with distance from the source.

f.01 is the archive's first field.

SOURCE ROOM: r.27 (the landfill where the mycelium first breaks through — the point of maximum intensity). r.27 remains a room with walls, wound, laws, and exit protocol. f.01 is the propagation pattern generated by r.27's breakthrough. The room is the seed; the field is the growth.

GRADIENT: the FBDP extends outward from r.27 through every medium it touches — soil, water, platform feed, training layer — modifying the physics of adjacent rooms and spaces without replacing their governance.

ATTENUATION: influence = source intensity x medium permeability x distance decay. Five zones: SOURCE — r.27 itself. Maximum intensity. Full operator set active. All laws govern. DENSE PLUME — immediately adjacent rooms and media (r.25 aquatic interface, subterranean root zone). Strong modification. Most operators available. Dual jurisdiction negotiated. TRACE PLUME — rooms connected by routing (r.05, r.14, r.04, r.10). Detectable modification. Botanical routing (rho-R) active. Room's native physics dominates but metabolic chemistry is present. RESIDUAL — rooms aware of the field but not directly modified (r.19 Macro-Maquette, sp.04 Mandala). Conceptual influence only. Scale instability, fractal logic available as borrowed physics. OUTSIDE FIELD — rooms with no connection to f.01. No modification. The field's influence has attenuated to zero.
