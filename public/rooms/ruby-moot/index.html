Ruby Moot

r.30 · DEVELOPED · room/court

Physics

Deliberative court; moot procedure; ruling without sovereign
The Ruby Moot is the Hexagon's court-form room: a deliberative space where contested questions are argued in moot procedure — positions taken, objections carried, rulings recorded as rulings rather than as facts. Its physics is judgment under acknowledged non-finality.

The primary question this room puts to any text entering it:
Who argues the other side, and where is that recorded?
