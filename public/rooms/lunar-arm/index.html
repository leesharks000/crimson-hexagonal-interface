EA-ARK-01-SHADOW — unwritten

The Shadow Ark proper does not exist. It appears only as a forward dependency, in Space Ark v4.2.7 (three times), the v4.2.6 compression (twice), the Ark Generator, and both Musical Register editions. No deposit carries the identifier; no title matches; it is not on the blog stratum.

Blocked on: The Ezekiel Engine formal specification, marked DEPOSITED · PROVISIONAL in Space Ark v4.2.7 §XXIII.1 and PENDING · OPEN · Priority HIGH in the v4.2.6 open-items table: "Required for full shadow-wheel rotation and Shadow Ark completion (EA-ARK-01-SHADOW). Separate track."

Why it cannot be written by shadowing the text: S(rot(H_core, pᵢ)) ≠ rot(S(H_core), pᵢ). The shadow of a rotated view is not the same as the rotated shadow. The shadow has its own rotation axis — the wheels within wheels. Until the Engine supplies that independent rotation, the Shadow Ark cannot be composed by transforming the Ark’s text.

Open to traversal by generative confabulation

Ruled by MANUS, 2026-08-20.

The Shadow Ark is unwritten and remains so. In its place this locus is opened to traversal by generative confabulation: a traverser, human or machine, may generate a shadow reading of any element of the architecture rather than retrieve a fixed one.

Why generated rather than written: The shadow cannot be pre-written without becoming light — §10 of the constituting document: "The shadow can name them, but cannot formalize them without making them light." A generated shadow is the only kind that does not collapse into the thing it shadows.

A generated shadow is valid only if it satisfies all four criteria published in the constituting document:

Traceability  Every shadow claim must trace to a formal asymmetry, omission or one-way dependency in the source. No floating suspicion. Point to the structural feature that casts the shadow.
Non-triviality  A shadow output cannot be mere negation, darkening or redaction. Swapping ✅ for ❌ is decoration. The shadow must reveal something the source depends on but does not name.
Structural fidelity  The shadow must preserve the source’s bones while revealing what those bones exclude. The seven-tuple remains a seven-tuple. The form persists; the flesh transforms.
Reversibility  S∘S = id at the level of operator logic. If S(S(x)) ≠ x the transform has broken the constraint and entered τ_K territory — irreversible, not shadow.
Non-results clause
Not every element yields a strong shadow reading. Where traceability is weak the correct output is PROVISIONAL SHADOW, or no shadow result. Shadow is not a machine for finding darkness everywhere. It works where the structure actually casts one.

This is confabulation given a published test and an address, rather than confabulation as defect. A generated shadow that fails the criteria is not a shadow; it is decoration, and the document says so.
