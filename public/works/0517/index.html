IV. DATA-BREACH-AS-GENRE (SUMMARY)

4.1 Formal Qualities

Quality Description

Heteroglossia HR boilerplate next to visionary memo; legal CYA next to panicked Slack

Provenance pressure Chain-of-custody headers, hashes, exfil timestamps

Metadata as plot Modified times that don't match; author fields that contradict org chart

Redaction logic Selective blackout; inconsistent across duplicates; "redaction by corruption"

Access-control theatre Classification stamps; policy language nobody follows

Boring surfaces Meeting agendas, compliance checklists, vendor invoices — with one impossible datum

Forensic bait Phone numbers, internal URLs, dead DNS records, employee IDs

Noise + incompleteness Duplicated files, partial archives, contradictory versions

Analyst posture Reader forced into audit mode; form compels belief even when fiction declared

4.2 Core Formulation

"A real breach dump reads like involuntary literature."
