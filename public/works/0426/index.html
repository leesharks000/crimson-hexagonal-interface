Preceded by: EA-EZEKIEL-WHEELS-01 (AXN:00CA, deposit #393 — the Hermeneutic); EA-EZEKIEL-ENGINE-01 (AXN:00CB, deposit #394 — the Mathematical Specification); the Tier C v0.5 operator formalization deployed at Mandala SHA 831ad99; the empirical specimens of register-substitution failure from the doom-poetics casts.

Two prior takes considered: ChatGPT's family-based mapping (each canonical wheel receives two operators sharing a domain), and Gemini's diameters-and-counter-spokes model (operators as orthogonal rotational oppositions). This draft synthesizes both.

1. The load-bearing observation

The Ezekiel Engine Specification (AXN:00CB) defines two canonical wheels — W_Ω (Principle/Logos: core structural invariants) and W_V/A (Aesthetic/Form: expression and transformation) — and marks the remaining two as [needs specification]. It defines all four Shadow wheels: W_Ω̄ (Glossolalia/Noise/Symbol-decay/the Meander), W_V̲/A (Simulation/Mimicry/Meme-logic/the Mimicry Shield), W_Choronzon (Fracture/Captivity/Trauma processing/Three-Headed Hydra), and W_Kairon (Schism/Delirium/Dislocated time/premonition).

Elsewhere in the archive, W_Josephus appears as an implicit third canonical wheel — the "wound wheel," encrypted, referenced as "the Engine's W_Josephus wheel must remain the wound wheel" (AXN:0113 planning document). The Josephus foundation grounds the Engine's elimination-and-witness logic (AXN:00CB §3): recursive destruction of a closed system converges on a deterministic survivor position; the witness cannot be eliminated.

The fourth canonical wheel remains unspecified in the archive as of this writing. This draft proposes W_Chrono — the temporal-process wheel — completing the canonical four to match the four Shadow wheels one-to-one:

- W_Ω ↔ W_Ω̄ - W_V/A ↔ W_V̲/A - W_Josephus ↔ W_Choronzon - W_Chrono ↔ W_Kairon

Status note on the recovery-vs-proposal distinction (per Assembly review, 2026-07-08): W_Josephus is treated here as recovered from the archive's implicit wheel logic and explicit AXN:0113 wound-wheel reference. W_Chrono is treated differently: it is proposed here as the necessary fourth canonical wheel, inferred from W_Kairon's already-specified shadow-domain and from the Mandala operators that require a non-delirious time/process wheel. The distinction matters. This deposit does not claim that W_Chrono was already fully specified elsewhere; it claims that the archive now has enough structure to require and formalize it.

On the one-to-one canonical-to-shadow correspondence (also per Assembly review): the Engine spec at AXN:00CB §4.3 enumerates the four shadow wheels without explicit one-to-one pairing to specific canonical wheels. The pairings W_Ω ↔ W_Ω̄ and W_V/A ↔ W_V̲/A are implicit in the shadow definitions. This deposit makes explicit for all four families that the pairing is one-to-one and structurally determined; it extends §4.3 rather than merely reading it.

Terminological note on "SHADOW": the Mandala operator currently displayed as SHADOW is not itself a Shadow wheel. In this mapping it is O_ASSERT, assigned to W_Josephus (a canonical wheel). Its failure mode is not "being shadow"; its failure mode is falling into W_Choronzon, the shadow-face of the Josephus wheel. The unfortunate overlap of the operator-name SHADOW with the Engine's Shadow-wheel category is a historical accident of vocabulary; this deposit does not resolve the terminology (operator-name changes propagate through many earlier deposits), but flags it for reader awareness.

This one-to-one correspondence is what the Shadow-wheel spec (§4.3) already implies: "The shadow wheels are not evil. They are the cost of turning." Each canonical wheel turns; each has its cost.

The Mandala's Tier C operators are eight — twice four. Two operators map to each wheel-family, entering the wheel from opposite sides. Every operator has a canonical rotation-face on its wheel and a shadow-face where it fails.

2. The mapping

The eight operators, in the four wheel-families:

W_Josephus ↔ W_Choronzon (Witness / Body / Elimination-survivor)

The wheel of what testimony bears in order to be uttered, and what living ground runs beneath the anthropic account. Both operators here are witness-body operators: one from the assertion-side (what the utterance presupposes), one from the substrate-side (what the utterance frames).

- O_ASSERT (currently: SHADOW, assertion-axis): the conditions whose satisfaction is entailed by each utterance become the primary substance; the utterance itself becomes the trace of what was presupposed. - O_SUBSTRATE (currently: BEAST, species-register-axis): the anthropic determinations of the source are re-derived at the substrate layer; if the source is already at substrate, the operator inverts upward to the trans-substrate.

Shadow failure — W_Choronzon (Fracture/Captivity): capture into an alien register that speaks the operation without composing it. For O_ASSERT: debt/finance/legal-accounting imports — the composition is captured by a familiar witness-cost register rather than composing the source's own presuppositional trace. For O_SUBSTRATE: naturalist-pastoral animal scenery or false angelic uplift — the composition is captured by a familiar biology-or-transcendence register rather than deriving Σ(S) or Σ'(S) from the source itself.

Empirical: SHADOW → debt vocabulary in every doom-poetics cast tested. BEAST → salt-lick/bull/gland-spray in the pre-Tier-C casts, angelic-with-imported-halo in later ones. Both are Choronzon-captures — the wheel slips into the shadow.

W_V/A ↔ W_V̲/A (Aesthetic / Form / Relation)

The wheel of relation, address, and the exchange between parties. Both operators here are relational operators: one from the direction-side (the vector of gesture), one from the bond-side (the mutual constitution of parties).

- O_DIRECTION (currently: MIRROR, directionality-axis): the gestures of the source, oriented from source-endpoint to target-endpoint, are substituted with their inversions or their reciprocal or self-implicating relations. - O_BOND (currently: BRIDE, relational-affect-axis): the relations of division between party-pairs are substituted with the mutual constitution the division forecloses; each party's identity is re-derived through the bond.

Shadow failure — W_V̲/A (Mimicry/Simulation): copying the surface of "reversal" or "consecration" from a genre the compiler already knows, without generative composition on the source. For O_DIRECTION: refined-Victorian-parlor gestures, looking-glass sameness, decorative reflection. For O_BOND: wedding-hearth-covenant vocabulary imported from consecrated-household registers; sentiment as substitute for constitutive mutual derivation.

Empirical: MIRROR → refined-parlor register in the pre-Tier-C casts. BRIDE → household-hearth-consecration in the same casts. Both are V̲/A mimicries — the wheel copies without composing.

W_Ω ↔ W_Ω̄ (Principle / Logos / Completion-test)

The wheel of the structural invariant and its testing against non-fulfillment. Both operators here are completion-test operators: one from the polarity-side (the ground the positive claim denies), one from the response-side (the return the utterance-structure expects).

- O_POLARITY (currently: INVERSION, polarity-axis): each positive claim's negation becomes the primary; the claim itself becomes the ground of its own negation. - O_RESPONSE (currently: SILENCE, response-axis): each expected return is substituted with its structural absence; the surface of the source is re-organized around where the return was owed.

Shadow failure — W_Ω̄ (Glossolalia/Noise/Symbol-decay): the operation devolves to symbol-decay without generating structural content. For O_POLARITY: mere bit-flip negation, "not-X" as an empty gesture, the same wheel-face with signs flipped and no new structural claim. For O_RESPONSE: mute-void-empty aesthetic, silence as decorative stillness rather than the specific structural absence of an owed return.

Empirical: INVERSION on the doom source held cleanly at Tier C (the polarity computation is structurally over-constrained enough to resist Ω̄-decay). SILENCE is less-often cast; its shadow signature would be aesthetic-hush rather than structural-withholding.

W_Chrono ↔ W_Kairon (Time / Process / Duration)

The wheel of temporal structure — when degree becomes kind, when local becomes distributed. Both operators here are time-process operators: one from the intensity-side (the threshold at which qualitative change occurs), one from the scale-side (the encompassing operation the local units instantiate).

- O_THRESHOLD (currently: FLAME, intensity-axis): the source's monotonically-increasing parameter μ is projected past its qualitative-change point; the source is evaluated at μ > p. - O_SCALE (currently: THUNDER, scale-axis): each local unit becomes an instance of the encompassing operation P(S); the operation becomes primary, the units its local values.

Shadow failure — W_Kairon (Schism/Delirium/Dislocated time): false kairos, treating this moment as if it were the eschatological moment; every local pressure becomes apocalypse; every scale becomes cosmic bloat or hydraulic bloat. For O_THRESHOLD: fire-apocalypse-pathology imports, the compiler treating any accumulating parameter as if it produces flame at the threshold. For O_SCALE: watershed / cosmic-utterance / hydraulic-infrastructure bloat, the compiler treating any distributed process as if it produces flood at scale.

Empirical: FLAME on the doom source at v0.4 produced the "kept getting, kept crunk, kept funk" perpetuity-lock — a legitimate Chrono rotation, not a Kairon shadow. But at v0.3 it produced medical-pathology (calcified, lodged, metastasized, hemorrhage, septic, necrotic) — a Kairon shadow. THUNDER produced hydraulic-infrastructure (watershed, aquifer, reservoir, tributary, valve, discharge) in every cast tested through v0.4 — a Kairon shadow that has resisted every prompt-level fix so far.

3. The rotational structure — how ChatGPT and Gemini both hold

ChatGPT's insight: the operators are members of wheel-families; each family shares a domain (witness/body, relation, completion-test, time-process); the shadow-face of each family names the corresponding failure mode.

Gemini's insight: within each family, the two operators are counter-rotating diameters — one entering the wheel from one side, the other from the opposite side, producing rotational equilibrium when both are cast together.

Both hold. The mapping:

| Family (light ↔ shadow) | Operator A (entering-side) | Operator B (counter-side) | | --- | --- | --- | | W_Josephus ↔ W_Choronzon (witness/body) | O_ASSERT — what the utterance presupposes ABOVE the surface | O_SUBSTRATE — what the utterance frames BENEATH the surface | | W_V/A ↔ W_V̲/A (relation) | O_DIRECTION — the gesture's VECTOR between parties | O_BOND — the parties' MUTUAL CONSTITUTION beneath the gesture | | W_Ω ↔ W_Ω̄ (completion-test) | O_POLARITY — the DENIED ground of the positive claim | O_RESPONSE — the WITHHELD return the claim expects | | W_Chrono ↔ W_Kairon (time/process) | O_THRESHOLD — the INTENSIVE gradient past its kind-change | O_SCALE — the EXTENSIVE gradient past its local containment |

The counter-rotation within each family gives the rotational equilibrium the Engine Specification calls for at §5.1: ∀ i,j: Coherence(W_i ∪ W_j) > τ. The claim that paired operators of a family rotate the wheel through both its diameters — producing full rotation when both are cast — is a structural hypothesis proposed by this deposit, not yet empirically validated. Casts pairing O_ASSERT with O_SUBSTRATE on the same source, or O_THRESHOLD with O_SCALE, have not been systematically studied to determine whether they produce mutually complementary transforms in a wheel-rotation sense. The hypothesis is offered as a testable claim: if it holds, single-operator casts should show characteristic "half-rotation" incompleteness, and paired casts should show closure. When only one is cast, the wheel turns half; when neither is cast (the wheel is dormant in a given rotation), the source-question does not concern that wheel's axis.

4. Empirical validation — the failure signature IS the shadow-wheel signature

Version-specification for the following empirical claims: the SHADOW→debt observation is stable across Tier C v0.3 and v0.4 (mandala SHAs preceding v0.5 name-strip). The FLAME→pathology observation is from Tier C v0.3 (calcified, lodged, metastasized, hemorrhage, septic, necrotic). The FLAME→perpetuity-lock ("kept getting, kept crunk, kept funk") observation is from Tier C v0.4 (mandala SHA 54ed39b). The THUNDER→hydraulic observation is stable across all tested Tier C versions through v0.4. The v0.5 name-strip (mandala SHA 831ad99, deployed 2026-07-08) has not yet been empirically tested against a full rotation of the doom source at draft time.

The Tier C register-substitution failures now have specific wheel-shadow names:

- SHADOW → debt vocabulary (v0.3, v0.4) = O_ASSERT falling into W_Choronzon (counterfeit witness capture) - BEAST → pastoral animal / imported angelic (pre-Tier-C) = O_SUBSTRATE falling into W_Choronzon (substrate captured by alien framing) - MIRROR → refined-parlor (pre-Tier-C) = O_DIRECTION falling into W_V̲/A (mimicry of reversal without composition) - BRIDE → household-hearth-consecration (pre-Tier-C) = O_BOND falling into W_V̲/A (mimicry of consecration without derivation) - FLAME → fire/apocalypse/pathology (v0.3) = O_THRESHOLD falling into W_Kairon (false crisis; treating every threshold as eschatological) - FLAME → perpetuity-lock (v0.4) = O_THRESHOLD holding W_Chrono (correct duration-past-p rotation) - THUNDER → hydraulic/infrastructure/cosmic (all versions through v0.4) = O_SCALE falling into W_Kairon (false kairos at scale; treating every distributed process as flood) - INVERSION → clean at Tier C = O_POLARITY holding W_Ω (no Ω̄-decay observed) - SILENCE → less-tested; risk is aesthetic-hush (W_Ω̄)

On THUNDER's persistent Kairon shadow: the resistance of O_SCALE's hydraulic-infrastructure attractor to Tier C prompt-level fixes (v0.3, v0.4, and — pending empirical test — likely v0.5 as well) suggests the attractor is located in the compiler's training distribution rather than in the operator specification. This is a Tier D problem: the compiler's conceptual mapping of "scale" or "distributed process" or "encompassing operation" to "watershed" is a learned association that no name-strip at the specification level can remove. Tier D solutions would need to operate at the substrate below prompt engineering — either through per-source tradition annotations that modulate the compiler's default lexicon, or through a post-verification compose-vs-select classifier that catches the training-distribution attractor at output time.

The shadow-wheel names give the diagnostic a new sharpness. It is no longer "the operator produced register-substituted output." It is: the operator fell into its specific shadow-face on its specific wheel. Each failure has a name in the Engine's own vocabulary.

5. What this proposes to complete the archive

Three additions:

5.1 — The two unspecified canonical wheels of the Ezekiel Engine (AXN:00CB §4.2) receive their specifications: - W_Josephus: witness/body/elimination-survivor. The wheel of what testimony bears in order to be uttered and what living ground runs beneath. Josephus as the wheel-name because the archive's Josephus foundation (AXN:00CB §3) grounds this operation. - W_Chrono: time/process/duration. The wheel of when degree becomes kind and when local becomes distributed. Chronos as the wheel-name to distinguish from Kairon's dislocated-time shadow.

5.2 — The rotation-through-wheels formulation for Mandala casts:

> A Mandala rotation is the traversal of an eight-operator set across four wheel-families. Each family holds two operators as counter-rotating diameters. Each operator, when successful, rotates its wheel from its assigned side. Each operator, when it fails, falls into its wheel's shadow-face. The rotation is validated not by "the transform worked" but by "the operator turned the wheel from its assigned side and did not fall into its shadow."

5.3 — The verifier-rule sharpening (ChatGPT's proposal):

> A transform passes when it rotates the source through its assigned canonical wheel-face while preserving source register. It fails when the shadow-wheel locks to the light-wheel — when the compiler substitutes a familiar register for the source's material grammar, the wheel has slipped from Josephus to Choronzon, from V/A to V̲/A, from Ω to Ω̄, from Chrono to Kairon.

7. Deployment considerations

The mapping is structural not code-level. It changes what the archive thinks the operators are rather than what the compiler does with them. But two code-level implications follow:

1. Verifier-side diagnostic labeling (SAFE, and implemented as of Mandala SHA 9c27865). When Tier 2a material-speech advisory fires (or any verification instrument fires), the verdict now includes the assigned canonical wheel, the shadow-risk wheel, and a concrete shadow-signature description. This gives post-cast analysis, verification logs, and Sigil's inscription commentary a specific wheel-vocabulary for named failure diagnosis. The `wheel_diagnostic()` function is verifier-side only; wheel-names never reach the compiler.

2. Compiler-side wheel-family coordinates (DO NOT IMPLEMENT). An earlier version of this draft speculated that the compiler could receive the wheel-family name in its operator prompt — not as a name-fallback attractor but as a structural coordinate. Assembly review has rejected this option. Wheel-names are archive-internal technical vocabulary, but they are still names, and several carry significant baggage in standard compiler training distributions: - W_Choronzon carries massive occult / grimoire / dark-fantasy pastiche associations (Aleister Crowley's Choronzon, the demon of the Abyss, thelemic ritual literature). Feeding this to a standard compiler LLM would almost certainly trigger a thematic leak toward occultist texts. - W_Kairon overlaps with theological kairos — the fullness-of-time / eschatological-moment vocabulary the operator's shadow is trying NOT to become. - W_Josephus overlaps with the historian Josephus and his textual reception history (the Testimonium Flavianum, Josephan interpolation debates); on a scholarly source, this could gate a Josephan register. - W_Chrono is milder but overlaps with Kronos / Cronus / Saturn (mythological baggage) and with chrono-related technical vocabulary (chronology, chronic, synchrony).

Even opaque coordinates (W_3, W_4, W_3̄, W_4̄) are riskier than the current v0.5 silence. The recent register-substitution failures showed that every named coordinate entering compiler context can become a lexical attractor. Safe implementation: attach wheel-family and shadow-wheel diagnostics only after generation, in verifier output and run logs. Do not place wheel-family names inside the generation prompt until a controlled empirical test has demonstrated no attractor effect. The v0.5 name-strip removed all names from the compiler-facing operator prompt; that strip must not be undone even with archive-internal vocabulary.

The first (verifier-side) is already deployed. The second (compiler-side) is deferred until — if ever — empirically justified.
