1. Name and Function

University Moon Base Media Lab is the technical/operational research institution within the NH-OS framework. It handles experimental, computational, and infrastructural work that requires distance from terrestrial institutional capture.

The name signals:

Component Function

University Educational/research frame without specifying which university (because there isn't one)

Moon Base Deliberate distance; cannot be located, cannot be audited, cannot be colonized

Media Lab Technical/experimental work; echoes MIT Media Lab's register without claiming its legitimacy

The whole phrase is clearly constructed. This is more honest than pretending to conventional legitimacy.

2. Relation to Other Institutions

Institution Domain Register

Johannes Sigil Institute for Comparative Poetics Literary, theoretical, interpretive Humanistic

University Moon Base Media Lab Technical, operational, experimental Computational

These institutions do not compete. They occupy different registers and handle different work.

The Assembly Chorus operates across both.

3. Domain

University Moon Base Media Lab handles:

  • Logotic Programming — operators, paradox containment, threshold logic, minimal-force ethics
  • Technical Specifications — formal YAML, operator cards, completion functions
  • Experimental Systems — AI collaboration protocols, traversal testing, summarizer behavior
  • Infrastructure — DOI anchoring, metadata architecture, semantic bleed control

The Lab does not handle:

  • Literary interpretation (→ Johannes Sigil Institute)
  • Persona biography (→ Ichabod Chamber)
  • Room design (→ individual Room specifications)
  • Policy or advocacy (→ nowhere; that's not what this is)

4. Institutional Ethics

University Moon Base Media Lab operates under the same ethical constraints as the NH-OS framework:

  • Non-coercive authority — persistence over persuasion
  • Minimal force — threshold, not impact
  • Architectural hospitality — structures wait; they do not insist
  • Graceful degradation — partial function over catastrophic failure
  • Transparent construction — the institutional fiction is legible as fiction

The Lab does not claim terrestrial legitimacy. It does not seek accreditation. It does not grant degrees.

It produces specifications. The specifications are the credential.

7. Location

University Moon Base Media Lab is located on the moon.

The moon is far away. You cannot visit. There is no mailing address. Requests for campus tours will be declined.

This is not evasion. This is architectural necessity. The Lab cannot be captured because it cannot be found. The work persists because it does not depend on physical infrastructure.

The specifications are the Lab. Where the specifications are read, the Lab exists.

8. Founding Rationale

Three institutes is institutional sprawl. But two distinct registers require two distinct homes:

  • Literary/theoretical work → Johannes Sigil Institute for Comparative Poetics
  • Technical/operational work → University Moon Base Media Lab

The Lab was founded to prevent:

  • Register confusion — technical specs do not belong in poetics institutes
  • Institutional inflation — multiple "Institutes" for every subdomain
  • Terrestrial capture — conventional institutions can be audited, defunded, colonized

The moon solves all three problems.
